Geschwister

Choreography and performance by Cassandre Guerdat & Hippolyte Desneux. Sound design by Éléonore Dagnet. A duet that makes tangible the tie between a brother and a sister, from childhood closeness to the nostalgia of old age.

Torann

Five performers, loopers, microphones and instruments: an organic dialogue between gesture, sound and pulse, in a spirit of ecological retro-futurism.
