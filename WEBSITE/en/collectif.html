Overview

Overview

Dance, music, stage and street

Collectif Hydres, founded by Cassandre Guerdat and Hippolyte Desneux, is based between Normandy and the Paris region. Their approach is deliberately cross-disciplinary, with dance in close dialogue with music and other forms of artistic expression.

Hydres questions the ties that run through people and the patterns of communication that shape human connection. Through organic, concrete movement, the collective moves with a rhythmic energy in perpetual motion, anchored in its environment.

Team

Artistic co-directors

Cassandre Guerdat

A cross-disciplinary artist from Rouen, dancer and choreographer. Trained at Université Paris 8 and then at Coline (Istres), she now performs for companies such as WLDN / Joanne Leighton. Artistic co-director of Hydres, she choreographs and performs in Geschwister and Torann, and leads the collective's administration, production and arts-education projects.

Hippolyte Desneux

Dancer, choreographer and musician, trained at R.I.D.C. in Paris and then at Coline (Istres). A performer with the company WLDN / Joanne Leighton, he co-directs Hydres artistically: choreographer and performer of Geschwister and Torann, he leads the collective's touring activity and sets up its cultural outreach workshops.

In-situ

Approach

A company built for any ground

A strong pull toward dance outside the studio runs through Hydres' research. The street, the city's uneven rhythms, its flows and encounters inspire and feed the collective's practice — to the point of making the surrounding soundscape a true partner in play.

Outreach

Training

Certified DP2i educators

Certified DP2i (Danseur·euse Professionnel·le Interprète Intervenant·e — professional dancer/teaching artist) after their training at Coline in June 2024, Cassandre and Hippolyte are qualified to design and lead arts-education programmes — more than fifty workshops delivered in middle and high schools to date.
