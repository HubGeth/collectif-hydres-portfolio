Quintet · Premieres November 2027

Torann

Torann

Notes of intent

Becoming a group, live

Five performers on stage, a set-up where loopers, microphones and musical instruments become partners in movement. Bodies compose and record, weaving an organic dialogue between gesture, sound and pulse in real time.

Festive, bouncing and energetic, this collective, cross-disciplinary performance is built on a series of sound experiments carried out directly on stage. The question at stake: how sound and movement relate across space and time, and how these individual bodily reactions try to "become a group".

Torann is conceived as an evolving form, playable in several versions with a variable number of performers and instruments — technically self-sufficient, on a stage as much as in a non-dedicated space.

  • Choreography & performance
    Cassandre Guerdat, Hippolyte Desneux, Maria-Raffaella De Franceschi, Johanne Charpe & Éléonore Dagnet
  • Duration
    50 minutes
  • Audience
    Ages 8+
  • Visual world
    Ecological retro-futurism — crocheted costumes, natural and recycled fibres
Set-up

Instruments & experimentation

Loopers, a vocal pedal, a drum machine, guitars, keyboards, violins, oboe, ukulele, microphones: the stage becomes an open studio. Each dancer amplifies their voice and breathing, and builds their own rhythmic loops — sound shaped live shapes the dramaturgy and the group's shifting states of body.
