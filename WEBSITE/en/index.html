The collective

Hydres questions
the ties that run
through us

Founded by Cassandre Guerdat and Hippolyte Desneux between Normandy and the Paris region, Hydres explores movement, rhythm and contact — through dance, music and other forms of expression, on stage as much as in public space.

Approach

A company built for any ground

The street, gardens, squares, art galleries, schoolyards: Hydres looks for the unexpected encounter, carried by the energy and unpredictable sounds of public space.

Outreach

Cultural outreach

Dancing, together, anywhere

Outreach workshops, school residencies, encounters with audiences: Cassandre and Hippolyte, certified DP2i dance educators, pass on their contact-based practice to every kind of public.
